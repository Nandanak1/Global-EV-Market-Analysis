# 🚀 <b>EV Market Analysis Dashboard</b>

<h3>📘 <b>Project Overview</b></h3>
This <b>Power BI dashboard</b> analyzes the <b>global electric vehicle (EV) market</b>, tracking <b>adoption trends</b>, <b>regional performance</b>, and <b>technology preferences</b>.<br>
It provides a clear view of how the EV industry is evolving — and where it's headed.

<hr>

<h3>🎯 <b>Objectives</b></h3>
• Track <b>global EV stock growth</b> and <b>sales trends</b><br>
• Compare <b>market share across key regions</b> (China, Europe, USA)<br>
• Analyze <b>adoption rates by vehicle type and powertrain</b><br>
• Evaluate <b>BEV dominance</b> in the electric vehicle market

<hr>

<h3>📈 <b>Dashboard Highlights</b></h3>
• <b>Total EV Stock:</b> 906k global electric vehicles<br>
• <b>BEV Share of Sales:</b> 82.8% of all EV sales are battery electric vehicles<br>
• <b>EV Share of New Car Sales:</b> 41.6% market penetration rate<br>
• <b>Global EV Sales (2015–2024):</b> Line chart showing growth trajectory<br>
• <b>EV Sales by Vehicle Type:</b> Breakdown of cars, vans, buses, and trucks<br>
• <b>Regional Analysis:</b> Comparison of EV stock and sales share by region

<hr>

<h3>🛠️ <b>Tools Used</b></h3>
• <b>Power BI</b> – Interactive dashboard and visualization<br>
• <b>Data Analysis</b> – Market trend identification and insights generation<br>
• <b>Data Storytelling</b> – Presenting complex market data in an accessible format

<hr>

<h3>📊 <b>Key Insights</b></h3>
• 🌎 Global EV stock reached <b>906k</b> – signaling market maturity<br>
• ⚡ BEVs dominate with <b>82.8%</b> share of EV sales<br>
• 🚗 EVs captured <b>41.6%</b> of new car sales – showing mainstream adoption<br>
• 🇨🇳 <b>China leads</b> in both EV stock and sales share among regions

<hr>

With the automotive industry rapidly transitioning to <b>electric</b>, this dashboard provides valuable insights for:<br>
• <b>Automakers</b> – to plan production strategies<br>
• <b>Investors</b> – to spot growth opportunities<br>
• <b>Policymakers</b> – to guide sustainable development<br>
